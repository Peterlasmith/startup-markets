About the desk

A weekly read, not a link dump.

Start Up Markets stores Peter’s Bleeding-Edge Markets briefs. Each Monday note is a Lightcone-style synthesis across top venture firms: where the frontier is heating up, which theses partners just put in writing, and who is worth a conversation that week.

The audience is a fast scan. Know what’s hot and who to network with. Skip the deal encyclopedia.

What every brief covers

  • Hot areas
  • Thesis watch
  • Who to network with
  • Capital signals
  • Cooling / crowded
  • Sources

The archive lists every week newest first. Tags are that week’s hot areas, so a theme can be followed from the index. Search runs over titles, tags, and the one-line summary.

A new week is a single Markdown file in content/briefs/. The newest brief sits at the top of the archive.

When that week also has an MP3, the brief page plays it and the same date shows up in the podcast. The memo stays the transcript. Any podcast app can subscribe with the RSS feed.
