## 1. Hot areas (3–5)

### A. Agent security & non-human workforce graphs
**Why this week:** Enterprises are past "should we deploy agents?" and into "who owns what these agents can touch?" On Sep 9, Sequoia doubled down on **Cymphony** ($25M Series A co-led with SMBC Fin Atlas Beyond Fund; ~$30M total funding; >$100M post-money). The product pitch is a **workforce graph** spanning employees, AI agents, and other non-human identities plus the systems/data they can reach — identity + data as one problem, not two. Sequoia partner **Bogomil Balkansky** told TechCrunch the firm already runs Cymphony internally; he also said if companies aren't spending on agent security over the next 5–10 years, he doesn't know what else they'll spend on. One day later (Sep 10), Greylock partner **Jason Risch** published *Rogue Agents: Deciphering Agent Intent* — arguing static permissions and prompt guardrails fail for long-running agents, and that the missing primitive is **delegation provenance** (human → agent → sub-agent → tool call) plus runtime behavioral baselines with a Visa-like "trust rate" constraint. YC's Fall 2026 RFS separately asks for **Proving You're Human** (deepfake/fraud trust layer). Same category family: trust infrastructure for a world where face and voice no longer prove identity.

**Network tilt:** security founders who think in runtime systems + model behavior (not CASB rules), and operators at F500s already deploying agents into production systems of record.

### B. Coding agents → governed "software factories"
**Why this week:** The category left "chat that writes code" and entered **fleet + control plane**. On Sep 15, **Factory** raised **$200M at a $5B valuation** (Blackstone, Khosla, Sequoia, Insight, NEA, others; >$400M total) to scale self-improving enterprise software factories — single governed system across the SDLC, including air-gapped/regulated deploy. Same week cluster: a16z's Marc Andreessen posted (Sep 9) on **Cognition**/Devin — agents writing more than 90% of Cognition's own production code (up from 13% a year prior); Cognition's own X post (Sep 8) cited a **>$2B raise at $48B**, led by a16z, Accel, Founders Fund, General Catalyst, and Avenir, with run-rate revenue from $492M (May) to almost $900M. a16z also led **Lightfield's $47M Series A** (Sep 9) — CRM rebuilt as a temporal context graph for humans *and* agents sharing one API. Pattern: winners aren't more copilots; they're systems of record + orchestration layers where agents are first-class workers.

**Network tilt:** technical founders who've shipped agent loops into real enterprise SDLC / GTM stacks; buyers are CTOs measuring agent effectiveness and token spend, not demo wow.

### C. Heterogeneous inference & the watts bottleneck
**Why this week:** Capital and thesis writing converged on **efficiency as net-new capacity**. a16z's Sep 4 Gimlet Labs memo (Raghu Raghuram, Sarah Wang, Shangda Xu, Stephenie Zhang) is blunt: "We are running out of watts." It frames five U.S. hyperscalers as expected to spend **$1T in capex next year**, NVIDIA mobilizing **>$500B** with capital partners for AI factories, and argues the next unlock is a **multi-silicon inference cloud** (GPU + CPU + specialty accelerators behind one API) — Gimlet claiming up to **10×** throughput/interactivity on frontier models in the same power envelope, with a frontier lab and a hyperscaler as customers. Bessemer Atlas's *AI Infrastructure Roadmap: Five frontiers for 2026* (structural but still the best public map) puts an **inference inflection** at the center and names Gimlet among heterogeneous-inference builders. YC Fall RFS goes further into the physical constraint with **Compute at Sea** (offshore modular compute flotillas).

**Network tilt:** systems/compiler/data-center operators, not just model researchers; anyone building routing, prefill/decode split, or edge inference for denied-comms environments.

### D. Defense + reusable space as "AI into the physical world"
**Why this week:** Soft capital narrative met hard dollars. YC's Fall 2026 RFS banner is *"AI is moving into the physical world,"* and for the first time includes a request authored by sitting **U.S. Army Secretary Daniel P. Driscoll** — low-cost interceptors, sensors, drones, resilient logistics, open-system hardware that survives extreme climates. On Sep 10, **Mach Industries** announced a **$600M Series C extension** doubling valuation to **$3.7B** in three months (prior June tranche $300M at $1.8B; investors include Ribbit, Infinite, Bedrock, Sequoia — Stephanie Zhan / Shaun Maguire as early Sequoia defense backers). On Sep 8, **The Exploration Company** closed a **$450M Series C** (largest-ever European space Series C) co-led by **Bessemer, Atomico, and EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund**; Bessemer partner **Alex Ferrara** joins the board; ~$680M total raised; >$2B in contracts/commitments. Index's Julia Andre (Sep 8) separately framed **Mistral's** latest mega-round as Europe's sovereignty + full-stack enterprise AI play — same geographic "build critical capability at home" mood.

**Network tilt:** dual-use hardware/software founders who can sell into open architectures and manufacturing bottlenecks (motors, energetics, propulsion), not just slide decks about autonomy.

### E. (Honorable / structural) Autonomous infrastructure engineering
Slightly earlier in the window but still hot: Sequoia-incubated **Empirik** spun out Sep 1 with **$21M** (Sequoia, Canapi, Alumni Ventures) as an "autonomous infrastructure engineer" — change as the fourth observability pillar. Balkansky again. Complements the agent-security thesis: if agents change infra at machine speed, you need both intent governance *and* change-risk graphs. Treat as adjacent heat to A and B, not a fifth equal category yet.

## 5. Cooling / crowded

- **Single-player AI chat wrappers.** YC's *Multiplayer AI* RFS is an explicit rebuke: agents that take hours/days of work were never meant to live in private threads. If the product is still "prompt box only you can see," you're late to the narrative partners are funding.
- **Agent security as a feature farm.** Balkansky openly said *scores* of companies are positioning here; incumbents (Microsoft, Okta, CyberArk, Wiz, Varonis) are expanding. Differentiation needs identity+data graphs or runtime intent — not another policy chatbot. Crowded *entry*, still open *architecture*.
- **Foundation-model mega-rounds as a SoftBank/strategic sport.** SoftBank bonding ~$11B for OpenAI tranche; Mistral's European mega-round pulled Index + strategics. For networking: assume frontier-lab equity is mostly closed to typical Series A relationships unless you bring distribution, sovereign compute, or eval/harness infra.
- **VC term sheets vs strategic M&A in hot AI apps.** Listen Labs walked from a signed Menlo **$125M** Series C at **$1.5B** to pursue Salesforce talks reportedly ~**$2B** (TechCrunch / Business Insider, ~Sep 9). Hot horizontal AI tools can exit to platforms before growth equity closes — founders should treat signed sheets as *not* the only path; operators networking into Salesforce/ServiceNow/etc. GTM matter more.
- **Crypto prices cold, YC contrarian.** Fall RFS *The Best Time to Build in Crypto* is deliberate bear-market advice (stablecoins, agentic commerce rails, institutional). Not a capital stampede this week — a partner *invitation* for non-consensus builders.
